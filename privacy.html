Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 31, 2026

Plain-language summary

The game has no ads, developer account, behavioral analytics, or in-app purchases. Progress, preferences, board placement, and optional diagnostics stay on your device unless you deliberately share a diagnostic file with support.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the game Warlords at the Gate: Tower Defense (the "Game") and this official website (the "Website"). It explains what information is processed when you use either service.

2. Information the developer does not collect automatically

The Game does not include developer-operated accounts, advertising, behavioral analytics, profiling, location tracking, or marketing trackers. The developer does not receive your voice, contacts, advertising identifier, device serial number, room imagery, hand-joint data, or precise room geometry.

3. Information stored locally by the Game

Campaign progress, medals, settings, preferences, and tabletop placement information are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to operate the Game and restore your chosen setup. It is not automatically transmitted to the developer.

You can remove local information by using available in-game reset controls or by deleting the Game and its local data through your device. Removing the Game may permanently remove local progress.

4. Mixed-reality and input data

Your headset and platform runtime process tracking, passthrough, controller, and hand-tracking information to provide mixed-reality gameplay. The Game uses the resulting runtime data locally for interaction and board placement. The developer does not receive or remotely store passthrough imagery, room scans, hand joints, or controller transforms.

5. Optional diagnostic logs

Diagnostic logging is disabled by default and may be enabled manually in Options. When enabled, bounded local log files may contain the application version, general platform category, level and wave events, gameplay outcomes, and performance counters.

Diagnostic logs do not include room imagery, room geometry, hand joints, voice, contacts, advertising identifiers, or device serial numbers. The Game does not upload these logs. The developer receives a log only if you deliberately send it when requesting support.

6. Store and platform services

PICO, Meta, Steam, or another store provider may process purchase, download, entitlement, account, device, achievement, or payment information under that provider's own privacy policy. The developer does not operate those store systems and does not receive your complete payment details.

If optional platform features are enabled in a future Game update, this Policy will be updated before the developer begins receiving or remotely processing any additional information. Developer-operated cloud saving is not part of the current release.

7. Website hosting

The Website is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not use a contact form, advertising, developer-operated analytics, or non-essential cookies. GitHub states that a visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes when a GitHub Pages site is visited. GitHub processes that information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Information you choose to provide

If you contact support, the developer may receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, screenshots, or an attached diagnostic file. It is used to answer the request, investigate the issue, maintain necessary support records, and meet legal obligations. Do not include passwords, payment information, room imagery, or other sensitive information in a support request.

9. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game has no social features, public profiles, user-generated content, or direct messaging. A parent or guardian who believes a child has voluntarily sent personal information to support may request its deletion through the public support contact on the storefront where the Game was obtained.

10. Retention and security

Local Game information remains on your device until it is reset, deleted, or removed through platform controls. Voluntary support communications are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, prevent abuse, maintain support history, or comply with law. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Your choices and rights

You control optional diagnostic logging and whether any diagnostic file is shared. Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to personal information you voluntarily send, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection. Store-account and payment requests should be directed to the relevant platform provider.

12. International use

GitHub and platform providers may process information in countries other than your own under their respective policies and legal safeguards. The developer does not independently transfer local gameplay, tracking, or room information to another country.

13. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the Game, Website, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes will be communicated through the Website, storefront, or Game update where appropriate.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning information voluntarily sent to the developer, use the public support contact shown on the storefront where you obtained the Game. Requests concerning a PICO, Meta, Steam, GitHub, or payment account must be sent to that provider.
